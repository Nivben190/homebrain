# 03 - Requests can't be re-addressed

**What to build:** A partner "Can you...?" request keeps its sender and recipient forever. Only the recipient can change its status (accept / done / decline); the sender can withdraw it. Today any household member can rewrite every column, including who sent it and who it's for.
