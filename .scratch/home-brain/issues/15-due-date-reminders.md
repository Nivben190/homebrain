# 15 - Due dates trigger reminders

**What to build:** Any item with a date property can remind you before it's due (the plan's item_due trigger). You choose how long before (at the time, 1 hour, 1 day...), and the reminder goes to the item's assignee or the space's audience.

**Blocked by:** 11 - One reminder sender

**Status:** done

- [ ] Setting a date on an item and a lead time sends a push at the right moment in your timezone
- [ ] Changing or clearing the date reschedules or cancels the reminder
- [ ] Marking the item done stops further reminders
- [ ] Tests cover scheduling, rescheduling and cancelling
