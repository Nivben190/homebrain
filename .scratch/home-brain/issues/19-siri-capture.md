# 19 - Siri capture to any space

**What to build:** "Hey Siri, Brain add milk" puts "milk" in the right space. A downloadable Shortcut asks (or is told) which space, and sends the text to the app with your hook token. Simple routing rules (e.g. food words go to To buy) are optional; picking the space explicitly always works.
